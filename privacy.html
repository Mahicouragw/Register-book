Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · RegisterBook Community
The short version. We collect your Google name, email and photo so other staff can find and recognise you, plus the books and messages you create. We never sell your data and we never share it with advertisers. It is stored in a Supabase database in the Mumbai (India) region. You can download everything, and you can delete everything.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy
From Google when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo So other staff can find you by name when they share a register, see who wrote a line, and recognise your photo in chats and calls.
What you create: register books, the lines inside them, dates, quantities and amounts That is the whole point of the app — it replaces your paper books.
Messages: private chats and group chats you send or receive So you and your colleagues can talk inside the app.
Notification details: if you switch notifications on, your browser gives the app a push “address” (a technical endpoint, not your phone number) and the app asks for your permission So you get a banner and a sound when someone messages, invites or calls you while the app is closed. You can switch this off at any time; we then delete that address.
Call signals: short technical messages used to connect an audio or video call between two phones So a call can ring and connect. The sound of the call goes directly between the phones where the network allows it.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or ad networks.
  • We do not use your books or messages to train anything.
  • We do not track you around the web. There are no third-party analytics scripts.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, your location or your phone number.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database in the Mumbai, ap-south-1 region. The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Your Google password is never seen by this app — signing in happens through Google.

Who can see your books

Nobody, until you share. A register you create is private to you. When you share it, the person (or the group) can read it. They cannot edit it. This is enforced by Row Level Security rules inside the database, so it holds even if someone bypasses the app. People in a group can see each other in the member list.

Keeping it

We keep your books and messages until you delete them. If you delete your account, your books, lines, shares, group memberships, messages and profile are removed. If a technical step of the deletion cannot be completed automatically, the request is recorded and a person completes it — and the app tells you honestly which of the two happened.

Your choices

  • Download everything: Settings → Delete my account → “Download my books first” gives you a plain text file of every book and line you own.
  • Stop notifications: Settings → Notifications → switch off. We delete the saved push address.
  • Remove a share: open your book → tap the person's name chip → remove.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm.

Children

The app is for staff use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Anything significant will also be shown inside the app.

Contact

Questions, or want your data exported or deleted by hand: numbersareplaying@gmail.com.
