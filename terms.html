Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · RegisterBook Community
The short version. RegisterBook replaces paper register books with shared digital ones. You sign in with Google, you own what you write, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time. Please do not put anything in here that the law requires you to keep on paper without checking first — for example a signed medical record.

1. Who may use the app

You must be a member of staff (or invited by one) and at least 13 years old. You sign in with a Google account, so the name, email address and profile photo shown in the app come from Google. Do not share your account with anyone else.

2. Your books and who can see them

  • You own what you create. A register you create belongs to you. Nobody else can edit it — not even the person running the app.
  • Sharing is view-only. When you share a register, the other person can read every page and search it, but cannot add, edit or delete anything. This is enforced in the database (Row Level Security), not just by hiding buttons.
  • Groups. If you share a register with a group, everyone in that group can read it. If you later remove someone from the group, they lose access.
  • If you delete a book you shared, it disappears for the other staff members too. The app warns you about this before you confirm.

3. Messages and calls

Private chats, group chats and audio/video calls are only visible to the people taking part. Calls connect directly between the two phones (peer to peer) where the network allows it. Do not use the app to send anything unlawful, threatening or harassing.

4. Notifications

If you switch notifications on, we send you a phone notification when someone messages you, sends you a friend request, invites you to a group, or calls you. You can switch them off at any time in Settings. On iPhone, Apple only allows web push after you use Share → Add to Home Screen; the app tells you this honestly.

5. Records that must stay on paper

Some records — for example certain medical, financial or legal registers — may legally have to be kept in a particular form or retained for a set number of years. Check your local rules before you stop using paper. You can export any book to a text file from Settings → Download my books, and you can print from your browser.

6. Availability

We aim to keep the app running, but it is provided “as is”, without warranty. Keep your own export if a book is critical. If a calling feature cannot connect, the app says so plainly rather than pretending.

7. Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account shows exactly what will be removed, lets you download your books first, and then deletes your account, books, lines, shares, messages and profile. This cannot be undone. If a technical step fails, the app records your request and tells you it is awaiting processing — it never claims your data is gone when it is not.

8. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms we will update the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the new version.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms: numbersareplaying@gmail.com.
